# Route card: practice-log

> **This is a routing card, not the skill.** The full, current rules live in the `open-brain-practice-log` skill. Once you have routed here, load `open-brain-practice-log` and follow its `SKILL.md` (and the references it names) end to end. Do not execute from this card.

## Route here when

- open-brain start / open-brain stop / open-brain harvest.
- Open, close or harvest a practice session.

## What the skill does

Opens, closes and harvests a practice session when you ask for it explicitly. `open` tags the domain and capabilities and surfaces supporting notes; `close` asks the closing questions, logs gaps and proposes Fleeting notes; `harvest` consolidates a domain's sessions into a brief (trained capabilities, recurring gaps, emergent capabilities).

## Route elsewhere when

- Reviewing sessions that never went through a practice log: **review**.

## Usual next step

- **escribir**, if the close produced a publishable fleeting idea.
- **synthesize**, if a pattern recurs across three or more sessions.
- **flashcards**, using the harvest brief.
