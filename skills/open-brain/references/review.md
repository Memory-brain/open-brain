# Route card: review

> **This is a routing card, not the skill.** The full, current rules live in the `open-brain-review` skill. Once you have routed here, load `open-brain-review` and follow its `SKILL.md` (and the references it names) end to end. Do not execute from this card.

## Route here when

- Weekly review / what did I do this week / what did I practice / summary of my sessions.

## What the skill does

Reviews your recent sessions (last N days), identifies what was done in each, detects patterns, and produces an activity brief: an activity table, patterns, and post ideas you did not capture. It is the source of truth for what you actually practiced, independent of whether a practice log was opened. Optionally saves ideas or the review note.

## Route elsewhere when

- Opening or closing a practice session: **practice-log**.
