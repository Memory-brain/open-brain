# Route card: ingest

> **This is a routing card, not the skill.** The full, current rules live in the `open-brain-ingest` skill. Once you have routed here, load `open-brain-ingest` and follow its `SKILL.md` (and the references it names) end to end. Do not execute from this card.

## Route here when

- Capture this / add it to the vault / save it in Obsidian / put it in the Inbox.
- Import or migrate content from Notion, a URL, pasted text, a PDF or a conversation.
- Record a real experience as a Case, or a passing idea as a Fleeting note.

## What the skill does

Entry gate of the vault. Triages raw content into the right note type (Inbox, Case, Fleeting, Evergreen or Design Reference), assigns a preservation tier, runs the SurpriseFilter novelty check, and wires the new note bidirectionally to its supporting notes (Mentors, Search Terms, Books). It files the source; it does **not** pull notes out of it.

## Route elsewhere when

- Turning a source into Literature Notes: route to **extract**.

## Usual next step

- **extract**, if the captured content is substantive.
- **autolink** runs silently after notes are created.
