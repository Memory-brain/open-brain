## What the skill does

Improves retrievability by adding search terms, domains and connections to existing notes without changing their content. An engine scans, audits and writes; the model generates the candidate terms. Optional phases: reactive enrichment driven by real usage evidence in the Retrieval Log (queries that failed or were reformulated), and typed connections added to Permanent Notes.
