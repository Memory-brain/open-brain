## What the skill does

Promotes convergent Literature Notes to Permanent Notes, and convergent Design References to Visual Pattern (PAT) notes. Holds the shared ritual: the convergence gate (at least two independent sources, plus one extension written by you per cluster), the endorsement state machine, and a silent update of the typed graph. Modes: chat (default), web UI, and visual. It never promotes on its own: you confirm what becomes permanent.
