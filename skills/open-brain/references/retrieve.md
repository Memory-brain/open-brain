## What the skill does

Retrieves vault notes to answer knowledge questions, find related notes, surface cross-domain connections and pull visual design references. Two tracks can run together: text (what you know about an idea) and visual (what form or pattern could represent it). It declares an intent mode (Operative, Exploratory, Critical, Expressive, Serendipity, Visual), logs every invocation in the Retrieval Log, increments usage counters on the notes it uses, and reinforces traversed graph edges (myelination).
