# open-brain-retrieve changelog

> Version history. It lives outside `SKILL.md` because it is history, not instruction: nobody needs it to retrieve; read it when you need to understand why a step is the way it is. Measurements quoted here were taken on the development vault during the skill's evolution; they justify the design, they are not claims about your vault.

---

## Changelog

- **v9.23.0** — **Queries about the system itself do not raise counters.** Step 11 gains an exception: challenge, audit, health or skill-design queries close the log with `--origen_uso auditoría` and do not increment `times_retrieved`; `log_entry.py close` records the read in `audit_retrievals` and does not myelinate. Reason: a periodic system challenge inflated dozens of counters with no real use.
- **v9.22.0** — Sidecar and myelination move into the engines: `eval_retrieval.py retrieve` writes the sidecar (with `--log-id`) and `log_entry.py close` records the traversals from `Notas usadas sustantivamente`. Reason: as manual steps, the sidecar got a handful of lines in weeks and myelination traversals went unrecorded for days at a time.
- **v9.21.0** — **The log entry is opened BEFORE searching (Step 0.1), not written afterwards.** A log written after answering depends on someone remembering to write it; newly mandatory fields (`Motor determinista`, the candidates sidecar) stayed essentially empty. When queries arrive through a client that leaves no local transcript, the agent's own record is the only possible one. Change: **Step 0.1** calls `retrieval-eval/log_entry.py open --query "<literal>"` before any search and keeps the `id`; **Step 10** stops typing the block and calls `close --id <id> --json fields.json`, which counts the wikilinks in the three lists, fills `Gaps`/`Juicio`/`Artefacto` with `ninguno` (mandatory with an escape value) and lists what was not recorded. An entry that is opened and never closed **stays in the log** with `**Estado**: abierta` ("status: open"); `log_entry.py coverage` reports open vs closed, so the logging gap is measured instead of assumed.
- **v9.20.0** — **Refactor: `SKILL.md` stops loading what is not used on every invocation.** The instructions weighed several times more than the notes a typical retrieve served. Everything that is not executable flow moved to `references/`: the changelog, the full Retrieval Log template, Query Types 2–9, the MRAgent reconstruction loop, the visual track, engine and sidecar detail, counters, and the evidence behind steps 0.8 and 0.9. Nothing was deleted. What stays in `SKILL.md`: the canonical flow, the gates (0.8 asks and blocks, 0.9 shows and does not block), literal commands, the log field schema and the prohibitions. Result: ~80 % fewer tokens per invocation.
- **v9.19.0** — **Step 0.8 goes from self-generated to asked.** The earlier measurement only compared expansion vs no expansion, never who writes it, so asking the user for the vocabulary contradicts no measurement (unlike 0.9, whose "do not ask" is backed by data). The step is now a blocking gate: the agent drafts 8–15 terms as before, presents them as a question and waits; tagged `(confirmed as is)` or `(user's terms)` in `Expansión`. Explicit fallback for non-interactive sessions: use the draft, tagged `(unconfirmed — non-interactive session)`. An implicit always-on retrieve stays silent by design.
- **v9.18.0** — Two changes: (1) **Query Type 9 — Attempt first (JIT telling)**: the user writes their answer in 2–3 lines before seeing the retrieval, and the skill reports the difference instead of answering directly — grounded in diagnosing the kind of gap before intervening and in desirable difficulties (Bjork); optional, never in pure Operative mode. New `attempt` field in the Retrieval Log when active. (2) **Step 0 now mentions the session's random note**, optional, if your Hot Cache generator writes one — a PN sampled with weight 1/(1+times_retrieved) over ALL active PNs, no confidence filter, unlike sleeping giants (confidence==high only).
- **v9.17.0** — **Step 4.8: candidates sidecar.** The Retrieval Log kept how many candidates there were but not WHICH ones or where they ranked — so one could not tell whether a relevant engine-only note never reached the pool (width) or reached it and was buried below the reading cut (dominance), which need different fixes. The step dumps the JSON Step 4.7 already returns to `Resources/Retrieval Candidates.jsonl`, append-only, one line per retrieve.
- **v9.16.1** — **Merge fix, no surface change.** Two versions were built on top of an older installed copy and, when installed, silently dropped Step 4.7, the `Motor determinista` field and the `ninguno (motor)` attribution. This version is the correct union. Lesson: **package from the source of truth, not from wherever it happens to be installed** — the diff does not fail, it just reintroduces an old version with new things on top.
- **v9.16.0** — Step **0.9: show the anchor before spending it**. On the 47 gold-labelled log queries of the development vault, **one concrete anchor from the user** raised recall@50 from 54.8 % to **77.0 %**; paired delta **+13.8 pp, 95 % CI [+3.6, +24.4]**, on top of the 0.8 expansion. It saturates at one, does not depend on the anchor being right, and no observable signal predicts where it helps — so the step **always shows** in five lines and never asks conditionally.
- **v9.15.0** — **`Expansión`** field in the Retrieval Log and step **0.8** that produces it. Translating the task into the vault's vocabulary before searching is worth **+16.9 pp recall@50** (37.9 % → 54.8 %), 95 % CI **[+7.8, +26.5]** — the only engine intervention whose interval does not cross zero. `Términos que matchearon` is **not** this: it is retrospective attribution.
- **v9.14.1** — `lsa.py` imported `scipy` at module level, so `eval_retrieval.py retrieve` aborted in any `python3` without scipy. scipy is only needed by `lsa.build()` (indexing); the per-query `LSA` class is pure numpy. The import is now deferred inside `build()`, with a `RuntimeError` naming what is missing. Querying works with numpy alone.
- **v9.14.0** — **The deterministic engine enters the formal retrieve.** On a judged pool the agent retrieved at ~97 % precision but ~31 % recall, and the engine found several times more of the pool's relevant notes than the agent alone. The gap is width, not tuning: the agent's triage is good but looks at few candidates. Changes: new **Step 4.7** (second candidate source, ~1.4 s, triage stays with the agent), **`Motor determinista`** field, and `ninguno (motor)` as a valid attribution.
- **v9.13.0** — **Full title in every log wikilink.** A large share of the log's wikilinks resolved to no note, many of them truncated with `…` or `...` because the short form of a title was reused in the gloss. A truncated link is an entry that can never be read back. The rule separates the identifier from the gloss.
- **v9.12.0** — Three new log fields: **`Tarea`** (closed vocabulary), **`Juicio que no estaba en el vault`** (what the user decided that no returned note said — the seed of a Case or PN that conversation otherwise loses) and **`Artefacto producido`** as mandatory with `ninguno` valid (so the log can be joined with an output ledger). `Términos que matchearon` also labels description hits explicitly (`description: "fragment"`).
- **v9.11.0** — New log field **`Términos que matchearon`**. Vaults accumulate many free search terms that appear in a single note; whether they help recall cannot be answered because the log recorded which notes came out but never what made them come out. With this field, the keep-or-prune decision becomes data after ~30 days.
- **v9.10.1** — The Level B `missing:` field was written but no skill or engine read it. Step 0.5 now consumes it: if a note repeats in `missing:` 2+ times in the last 10 entries, flag it before answering.
- **v9.10** — Classifier fix: rule 6 (Visual) ran as a sequential fallback behind rules 2–5, so any query containing "challenge" or "designer" was classified Critical and Visual Retrieval never fired. Rule 6 is now always evaluated, in parallel, and can run as an additional track. Trigger widened to the "design" stem plus "interface", "UI", "visual coherence".
- **v9.9** — fair domain weighting across domains. **v9.8** — serve only top-K instead of every match (−45 % tokens). **v9.7** — Hot Cache self-healing fallback. **v9.6** — bilingual probe discipline and extension of the loop to Literature Notes. **v9.5** — guidance mode and `tokens_served`. **v9.4** — myelination.
