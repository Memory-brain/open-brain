## Delegate the decision right together with the task

Hartigan distinguishes handing over work from handing over decisions. When only the work moves, every open question travels back to the manager, and the manager becomes the bottleneck. He recommends naming, at hand-off, which decisions the person can make alone.

> "If they have to ask, you did not delegate; you outsourced typing." — Dov Hartigan, Hartigan, 2021, ch. 3

**Evidence**: In his own team, writing the decision list into each hand-off cut the questions waiting on him from a dozen a week to two or three.

**Source pointer**: Hartigan, 2021, ch. 3.
