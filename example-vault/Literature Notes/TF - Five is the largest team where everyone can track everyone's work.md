## Five is the largest team where everyone can track everyone's work

Hartigan's central claim is that five is a ceiling, not a target: with five people each member can hold the others' current work in mind, so coordination happens in passing. A sixth person tips the team into needing a status meeting.

> "The sixth person does not add a sixth of the work; they add a meeting." — Dov Hartigan, Hartigan, 2021, ch. 1

**Evidence**: Across the eleven teams he managed, the number of recurring meetings stayed flat up to five members and roughly doubled at seven.

**Source pointer**: Hartigan, 2021, ch. 1.
