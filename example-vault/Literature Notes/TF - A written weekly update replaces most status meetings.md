## A written weekly update replaces most status meetings

Hartigan replaced his team's daily standup with a written update: three lines per person every Friday, done, next, blocked. The meeting time went to pairing. He insists the update needs a named reader or it decays into a write-only ritual.

> "Status is information, and information travels better on paper than in a circle." — Dov Hartigan, Hartigan, 2021, ch. 4

**Evidence**: After the switch, his team's meeting hours fell from about six to two per person per week, with no rise in missed hand-offs over the following quarter.

**Source pointer**: Hartigan, 2021, ch. 4.
