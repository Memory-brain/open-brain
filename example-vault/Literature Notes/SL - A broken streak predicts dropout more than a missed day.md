## A broken streak predicts dropout more than a missed day

Ellwood observes that people rarely quit because of one missed day. They quit because a visible counter dropped to zero and the restart felt like starting from nothing. She suggests tracking a weekly count instead of a consecutive-day streak.

> "Nobody quits on day one of a slump; they quit on the day the counter resets." — Mara Ellwood, Ellwood, 2019, ch. 6

**Evidence**: Volunteers using a consecutive-day counter were more likely to stop logging entirely after their first miss than volunteers who tracked a weekly total.
