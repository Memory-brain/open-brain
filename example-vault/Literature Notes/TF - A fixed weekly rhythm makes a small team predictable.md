## A fixed weekly rhythm makes a small team predictable

Hartigan argues that a small team gains more from a fixed weekly rhythm (plan Monday, update Friday) than from daily check-ins. The week is long enough to finish something and short enough to notice drift.

> "A week is the smallest unit in which a team can both finish something and notice it is lost." — Dov Hartigan, Hartigan, 2021, ch. 6

**Evidence**: Teams on a fixed weekly rhythm hit their own estimates more often in his tracking than teams that re-planned daily.

**Source pointer**: Hartigan, 2021, ch. 6.
