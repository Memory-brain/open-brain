## A team ritual survives only with a single named owner

Hartigan lists the rituals his teams dropped and finds one shared trait: nobody owned them. A retrospective 'owned by the team' is skipped once and never restarted. His fix is to give each ritual one owner whose job includes cancelling it when it stops being useful.

> "Everybody's ritual is nobody's ritual." — Dov Hartigan, Hartigan, 2021, ch. 5

**Evidence**: Of the fourteen recurring rituals he tracked over two years, all five that lasted had a named owner; none of the ownerless ones survived a quarter.

**Source pointer**: Hartigan, 2021, ch. 5.
