## Automaticity tracks repetitions, not calendar days

Ellwood argues that the common question "how many days until it sticks?" is the wrong unit. What builds automaticity is the number of times the behavior is performed in the same context. Someone who runs the routine twice a day gets there in roughly half the calendar time of someone who runs it every other day.

> "Count the loops, not the days." — Mara Ellwood, Ellwood, 2019, ch. 2

**Evidence**: In her diary study of 140 volunteers, the number of logged repetitions explained most of the variation in self-reported automaticity; days since start added almost nothing once repetitions were known.

**Source pointer**: Ellwood, 2019, ch. 2.
