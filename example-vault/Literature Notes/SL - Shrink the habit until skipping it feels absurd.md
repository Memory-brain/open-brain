## Shrink the habit until skipping it feels absurd

Ellwood's rule is to design the first version of a habit for the worst day of the week, not the best one. One page instead of one chapter, one push-up instead of a workout. The full version can follow the small one; the reverse almost never happens.

> "Design for the Tuesday you are sick, not the Saturday you are keen." — Mara Ellwood, Ellwood, 2019, ch. 4

**Evidence**: Among volunteers who started with a 'full' version, most dropped out within three weeks; most of those who started with a deliberately tiny version were still logging repetitions at eight weeks.

**Source pointer**: Ellwood, 2019, ch. 4.
