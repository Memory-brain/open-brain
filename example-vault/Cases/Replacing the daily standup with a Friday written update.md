## Situation
Our four-person team spent about half an hour a day in a standup that mostly repeated what was already in the tracker.

## What happened
We replaced it with three written lines per person every Friday (done, next, blocked). The first two weeks nobody read the updates and two hand-offs slipped. In week three one person took ownership: she read every update on Friday afternoon and replied to each blocked line.

## What I learned
The written format was not the fix; the named reader was. Without an owner the update was a write-only ritual, exactly the failure in [[TF - A team ritual survives only with a single named owner]].
