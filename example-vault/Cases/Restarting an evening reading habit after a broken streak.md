## Situation
I had read every evening for 40 days, tracked with a streak counter in a phone app.

## What happened
Two sick days reset the counter. I did not open a book for three weeks. I restarted with a weekly page count reviewed on Sundays, and have not had a gap longer than four days since.

## What I learned
The habit was already small (one paragraph counted), so size alone did not protect it: the streak counter did the damage. That is a limit on [[A new routine sticks when it is small enough to run on a bad day]], and support for [[Judge a practice by its weekly review, not by its daily streak]].
